# Class feature mechanics

Class features in `src/2014/en/5e-SRD-Features.json` state their rules as text (`desc`). Six optional fields in
`feature_specific` restate the mechanics of that text as data:

| Field                   | What it says                                                                  | Features |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| `engine`                | What using the feature costs, how it recharges, its uses, and what it changes | 187      |
| `heal`                  | The hit points the feature restores                                           | 9        |
| `granted_proficiencies` | The proficiencies the feature gives                                           | 5        |
| `grants_asi`            | `true` on every Ability Score Improvement                                     | 63       |
| `granted_languages`     | The languages the feature gives                                               | 15       |
| `damage_type`           | The damage type of a dragon ancestor                                          | 10       |

They are additive: no existing field, record or id is changed, and the existing `feature_specific` fields
(`expertise_options`, `subfeature_options` and the others) keep their place. They are English-only (nothing in them
is translated, so the translation overlays do not carry them) and marked `dd` in `src/provenance/source-markers.json`.

`engine`, `heal`, `granted_proficiencies` and `granted_languages` are written by hand, one record for each feature, in
`src/2014/feature-engine-annotations.json` (the feature texts have no fixed wording to parse) and merged into the
features by `src/2014/derive-feature-engine.ts` (`pnpm run derive:features`); `grants_asi` is set by the same script on
every feature named Ability Score Improvement, and `damage_type` from the name of each dragon ancestor. The Zod schema in `src/2014/schemas/5e-SRD-Features.ts` fixes the
vocabulary below. `src/2014/tests/feature-engine.test.ts` checks the values against the feature text and the Levels
rows, with rules that do not share code with the derivation.

The `engine` and `granted_proficiencies` fields are also on feats, in `src/2014/en/5e-SRD-Feats.json`, with the same
shape and the same vocabulary; see [feat-data.md](feat-data.md). The feats add a few words, marked below as used by feats
only, so the counts in this document are those of class features.

## Scope

`engine` covers the features of every class level, 1 to 20, in the twelve classes and their SRD subclasses. Levels 1 to
5, the levels at which a character starts, are in scope for every class; levels 6 to 20 are in scope for the classes
named in `ENGINE_MAX_LEVEL_BY_CLASS` of `derive-feature-engine.ts`, which are now all twelve: the barbarian, fighter,
monk, rogue, paladin, cleric, wizard, bard, druid, sorcerer, ranger and warlock, with their subclasses (the Oath of
Devotion, the Life domain, the school of Evocation, the College of Lore, the Circle of the Land, the Draconic
Bloodline, the Hunter and the Fiend among them). That is all 407 features, and 187 of them have an `engine`: 99 at
levels 1 to 5 and 88 at levels 6 to 20. Of the other 220, 86 have only `grants_asi` (the Ability Score Improvements),
only `heal`, only `granted_proficiencies` (Bonus Proficiency, Bonus Proficiencies, Beguiling Influence, Slippery Mind),
or only `granted_languages` (Druidic, Thieves' Cant, the three Favored Enemy records) or `granted_languages` and
`damage_type` (the ten dragon ancestors), and 134 have no mechanic these fields can state. 137 features are listed
with the reason in `ENGINE_EXCLUDED` of `derive-feature-engine.ts` and in
[What is not structured](#what-is-not-structured) (the three Favored Enemy records have `granted_languages` and are
listed as well). The test fails if a feature in scope has no structured field and is not listed, and if a feature
outside the scope has an `engine`.

`heal`, `granted_proficiencies`, `granted_languages`, `damage_type` and `grants_asi` cover every level.

A feature that the SRD splits into one record per count or die (`action-surge-2-uses`, `indomitable-3-uses`,
`brutal-critical-2-dice`, `extra-attack-3`, `bardic-inspiration-d8`, `wild-shape-cr-1-2-or-below-no-flying-speed`) has
the same `engine` on each record in scope. Features with the same text have the same `engine`: the fighting styles of
the fighter, paladin and ranger, the Indomitable, Brutal Critical, Action Surge and Extra Attack records, the cleric's
three Channel Divinity and four Destroy Undead records, the four Bardic Inspiration and three Wild Shape records of the
bard and the druid, and the four Mystic Arcanum records.

An owner has several records of such a feature at the higher levels (a 17th-level fighter has `indomitable-1-use`,
`indomitable-2-uses` and `indomitable-3-uses`), and they restate one feature, so read them once. A number that comes
from a Levels key is the owner's total at the owner's level and is not added up across the records: the uses of Action
Surge and Indomitable, the dice of Brutal Critical. Extra Attack is the exception: each record has `count: 1` and the
extra attacks of the records the owner has add up (one at 5th level, two at 11th, three at 20th), as its text states
them one record at a time. The Channel Divinity and Destroy Undead records of the cleric are totals like Indomitable:
read `channel_divinity_charges` and `destroy_undead_cr` once, at the owner's level.

## Levels `class_specific`

Where the class table gives a number that grows with the level, the feature names its key in the `class_specific` of
the class rows of `5e-SRD-Levels.json` instead of repeating the numbers. The value is read from the row of the
feature's class at the owner's level in that class. The test checks that each key exists in the rows of the class and
that a key used for `uses` is above 0 at the feature's level.

| Key                                                      | Read by                                                 | Value                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `rage_count`                                             | Rage `uses`                                             | Rages before a long rest; 9999 at 20th level (unlimited)                     |
| `rage_damage_bonus`                                      | Rage `bonus`                                            | Melee damage bonus while raging                                              |
| `bardic_inspiration_die`                                 | Bardic Inspiration, Cutting Words, Peerless Skill `die` | Faces of the die (6 is a d6)                                                 |
| `channel_divinity_charges`                               | Channel Divinity (cleric) `uses`                        | Uses between rests                                                           |
| `destroy_undead_cr`                                      | Destroy Undead `destroy`                                | Highest challenge rating destroyed                                           |
| `wild_shape_max_cr`, `wild_shape_fly`, `wild_shape_swim` | Wild Shape `wild_shape`                                 | Highest challenge rating, and whether flying and swimming beasts are allowed |
| `action_surges`                                          | Action Surge `uses`                                     | Uses between rests                                                           |
| `indomitable_uses`                                       | Indomitable `uses`                                      | Uses between long rests                                                      |
| `brutal_critical_dice`                                   | Brutal Critical `critical_damage_dice`                  | Additional weapon damage dice on a critical hit with a melee attack          |
| `ki_points`                                              | Ki `uses`                                               | Ki points                                                                    |
| `martial_arts`                                           | Martial Arts `damage_die`                               | `{ dice_count, dice_value }`                                                 |
| `unarmored_movement`                                     | Unarmored Movement `speed`                              | Feet added to the speed                                                      |
| `sneak_attack`                                           | Sneak Attack `bonus`                                    | `{ dice_count, dice_value }`                                                 |
| `sorcery_points`                                         | Font of Magic `uses`                                    | Sorcery points                                                               |
| `creating_spell_slots`                                   | Flexible Casting `uses` and `spell_slot_conversion`     | `[{ spell_slot_level, sorcery_point_cost }]`                                 |
| `arcane_recovery_levels`                                 | Arcane Recovery `recover_spell_slots`                   | Combined level of the slots recovered                                        |
| `aura_range`                                             | The paladin auras, in `aura`                            | Range of the auras in feet: 10 from 6th level, 30 from 18th level            |

## `engine`

`{ action_cost, recharge?, uses?, modifiers? }`. Every `engine` has an `action_cost`, and at least one of a
`uses`, a `modifiers` or an `action_cost` other than `passive`.

### `action_cost`

| Value          | Meaning                                                                                                                    | Features |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| `action`       | Using the feature takes an action                                                                                          | 39       |
| `bonus_action` | It takes a bonus action                                                                                                    | 15       |
| `reaction`     | It takes a reaction                                                                                                        | 10       |
| `free`         | It is used on purpose but takes no action: at a moment the text names, such as when the owner hits, rages or casts a spell | 38       |
| `passive`      | It is never used on purpose: it applies while owned, or it is a pool that other features spend                             | 81       |
| `rest`         | It is used while the owner finishes a rest (a short rest, or a long rest for Tranquility)                                  | 4        |

A feature that only casts a spell (`spell` modifier) takes the spell's casting time: `action` for the at-will
invocations, `passive` when the casting time is longer than an action; except when it is used at the end of a rest
(`rest`, Tranquility).

### `recharge`

When spent uses come back. Present exactly when `uses` is a count of the feature's own.

| Value        | Meaning                              | From the text                                 | Features |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| `short_rest` | At the end of a short or a long rest | `short or long rest`                          | 14       |
| `long_rest`  | At the end of a long rest            | `long rest`                                   | 27       |
| `day`        | Once a day                           | `Once per day` (Arcane Recovery)              | 1        |
| `turn`       | At the start of each turn            | `once per turn`, `once on each of your turns` | 5        |

### `uses`

One of three shapes.

| Shape                                                                                          | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Features |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------- |
| `{ count?, class_specific?, by_level?, ability_modifier?, per_class_level?, minimum?, unit? }` | The feature's own uses: `count` plus the `class_specific` number plus the ability modifier plus `per_class_level` times the class level, and at least `minimum`. `unit` is `use` (when absent), `point` or `hit_point`   | 47       |
| `{ pool, cost?, cost_class_specific? }`                                                        | Each use spends `cost` from the uses of the feature `pool` (an index). `cost` is a number, or `spell_level` (the spell's level, 1 for a cantrip). `cost_class_specific` names a table of costs in the Levels row instead | 22       |
| `{ spell_slot: true }`                                                                         | Each use expends one of the owner's spell slots                                                                                                                                                                          | 3        |

Examples: Rage `{ class_specific: "rage_count" }`; Bardic Inspiration `{ ability_modifier: "cha", minimum: 1 }`;
Divine Sense `{ count: 1, ability_modifier: "cha" }` (1 + the Charisma modifier); Lay on Hands
`{ per_class_level: 5, unit: "hit_point" }`; Ki `{ class_specific: "ki_points", unit: "point" }`; Flurry of Blows
`{ pool: "ki", cost: 1 }`; Turn Undead `{ pool: "channel-divinity-1-rest", cost: 1 }`; Divine Smite
`{ spell_slot: true }`.

A pool is the lowest-level record of its feature (`bardic-inspiration-d6`, `channel-divinity-1-rest`); the
higher-level records of the same feature hold the same pool. The pool features are Ki, Font of Magic, Channel Divinity
(cleric and paladin) and Bardic Inspiration. Peerless Skill spends a use of Bardic Inspiration and Draconic Presence 5 sorcery points; Elemental Affinity's cost is on the resistance it gives (see `damage`).

Counts: `bonus` 31, `spell` 20, `extra_attack` 14, `condition` 13, `spell_change` 13, `immune_to` 7, `roll` 6, `die` 6,
`destroy` 5, `actions` 5, `reroll` 4, `reduce_damage` 4, `damage` 4, `armor_class` 3, `critical_damage_dice` 3,
`recover_uses` 3, `wild_shape` 3, `evasion` 3, `rolls_against_owner` 3, `speed` 2, `turn` 2, `recover_spell_slots` 2,
`reroll_damage` 2, `rolls_against_others` 2, `push` 2, `spell_slot_conversion` 2, `protect_from_spell` 2,
`critical_range` 2, `deal_damage` 2, and one each of `recharge`, `attack_ability`, `damage_die`, `hit_point_maximum`,
`temp_hp`, `avoid_dropping_to_zero`, `minimum_d20`, `minimum_total`, `ability_score_increase`, `magical_attacks`,
`end_condition`, `end_spell`, `fly_speed` and `unlimited_uses`. 162 features have `modifiers`.

**`bonus`.** `to` is `attack_roll`, `damage_roll`, `saving_throw`, `armor_class`, `ability_check` or `healing` (the
hit points a healing spell restores). A bonus has exactly one amount:

| Amount                    | Meaning                                                                  | Example                                               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `flat`                    | A number; with `add_spell_level`, plus the spell's level                 | Archery `2`; Disciple of Life `2` + the spell's level |
| `dice`                    | Dice                                                                     | Colossus Slayer `1d8`                                 |
| `dice_at_slot_level`      | Dice for each spell slot level spent, keyed `"1"` to `"5"`               | Divine Smite `2d8` to `5d8`                           |
| `dice_at_class_level`     | Dice from the class level in the key, the highest key reached applies    | Divine Strike `1d8` at 8, `2d8` at 14                 |
| `ability_modifier`        | The modifier of that ability                                             | Agonizing Blast `cha`                                 |
| `attack_ability_modifier` | `true`: the modifier of the ability the attack uses                      | Two-Weapon Fighting                                   |
| `class_specific`          | The number or dice of that Levels key                                    | Rage `rage_damage_bonus`, Sneak Attack `sneak_attack` |
| `proficiency_multiplier`  | The proficiency bonus times this, rounded as `round` (`down`, `up`) says | Jack of All Trades `0.5`, `down`                      |

`abilities` limits a bonus to attacks or checks that use one of those abilities, `damage_type` gives the extra damage that type,
`spell` limits it to one spell, `min_spell_level` to spells of that level or higher and `school` to spells of that
school (indexes of `5e-SRD-Magic-Schools.json`); `minimum` is the least the amount can be (Aura of Protection's +1).
`target: "self"` gives the bonus to the owner and not to the creature the roll is about: Blessed Healer heals the owner
by 2 plus the spell's level when the owner's healing spell restores hit points to another creature, which the text
states and the record leaves out.

**`aura`.** A `bonus`, `roll`, `condition`, `immune_to` or `deal_damage` modifier can have
`aura: { class_specific | range_ft, affects, must_hear_owner? }`: it applies to the creatures within that many feet of
the owner instead of to the owner alone (a `condition` with an `aura` is put on those creatures, `target: "target"`). The range is the number of
feet in the `class_specific` key of the Levels row (`aura_range`: 10 feet from 6th level, 30 feet from 18th level, so
Aura Improvements has no record of its own), or `range_ft` for a fixed radius (Holy Nimbus, 30; Countercharm, 30;
Draconic Presence, 60). `affects` is `allies_and_self` (the owner and the friendly creatures) or `enemies` (the
creatures hostile to the owner). `must_hear_owner: true` limits it to creatures that can hear the owner (Countercharm).
The test reads the key at the owner's level and checks that the text gives that range.

**`spell_change`.** `maximize_healing_dice` gives every die of a healing spell its highest number (Supreme Healing);
`half_damage_on_successful_save` makes a creature that succeeds on its saving throw take half the damage and none of the
other effects (Potent Cantrip, `max_spell_level: 0`: cantrips only). `max_spell_level` limits the
change to spells up to that level, 0 being a cantrip. `no_material_without_cost` drops the material components that have
no cost and that the spell does not consume, as the text of Archdruid says; with `no_verbal_or_somatic`, which the
Metamagic option Subtle Spell uses for one casting, it states that the owner ignores the components of the spell.

**`rolls_against_owner`.** `no_advantage: true` is Elusive; `mode: "disadvantage"` with `creature_types` is the attack
rolls of creatures of those types, as the protection from evil and good spell has them, which Purity of Spirit names with
`spell` and `always_active: true`; `mode: "disadvantage"` with `opportunity_attacks_only: true` is the opportunity
attacks (Escape the Horde).

**`roll`.** `against_conditions` limits a saving-throw modifier to the saving throws against being given one of those
conditions (Countercharm and Steel Will: `frightened`, and `charmed`), and an `aura` gives it to the creatures of the
aura.

**`damage`.** The types are listed in `types`, or `types_from: "dragon_ancestor"` takes them from the `damage_type` of
the dragon ancestor record the owner chose (Elemental Affinity, whose resistance is to the damage type of the owner's
draconic ancestry). `cost` is what a use of the modifier spends from the `uses` of another feature, in the shape of the
`pool` form of `uses`: the 1 sorcery point of Elemental Affinity's resistance is a cost of that modifier and not of the
feature, because its damage bonus costs nothing. `bonus` has the matching limit `spell_damage_type_from:
"dragon_ancestor"`: only spells that deal damage of that type.

**`die`.** `add_to_own` is the die the owner adds to the owner's own ability check (Peerless Skill), and has no
`range_ft`.

**`immune_to`.** `creature_types` limits the immunity to the conditions that creatures of those types cause (Nature's
Ward: charmed and frightened by elementals and fey). Its immunity to poison is stated like Purity of Body's, with
`poisoned` in an `immune_to`, `disease`, and an immunity to poison damage; the `poisoned` condition names both features
in `immune_if_feature`.

**`fly_speed`.** `equal_to_speed: true`: a flying speed equal to the owner's current speed (Dragon Wings).
**`unlimited_uses`.** The owner can use these features, which are Wild Shape records, any number of times (Archdruid).

**`save`.** `{ ability, dc }`: the ability of the saving throw, and `dc` is the save DC:

| `dc`                                   | Meaning                                                                                                                                                   | Used by                               |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| `spell`                                | The owner's spell save DC, as Channel Divinity says                                                                                                       | Turn Undead                           |
| `ki`                                   | The ki save DC                                                                                                                                            | Stunning Strike, Open Hand Technique  |
| `{ ability_modifier }`                 | 8 plus the proficiency bonus plus the modifier of that ability, as the text says it                                                                       | Intimidating Presence, Tranquility    |
| `{ fixed, increase_per_use?, reset? }` | A number that rises by `increase_per_use` each time the owner uses the feature again, and returns to `fixed` when the owner finishes a short or long rest | Relentless Rage (10, 5, `short_rest`) |

`spell` modifiers take a `save_dc` of the second form when the spell's save DC is not the owner's spell save DC.

**`duration`.** `{ unit, length? }`: `unit` is `round`, `minute`, `hour` or `end_of_next_turn` (until the end of the
owner's next turn), and `length` is the number of units.

**`bonus.to: "initiative"`.** The initiative roll (feats only: Alert adds the proficiency bonus, `proficiency_multiplier: 1`).

**`roll`.** `roll` is `attack`, `ability_check`, `saving_throw`, `death_saving_throw` or `initiative`, and `mode` is
`advantage`, `disadvantage` or `auto_fail`, as in condition `effects.rolls`. `skills` limits an ability check to the
checks of those skills (indexes of `5e-SRD-Skills.json`) and `abilities` to checks of those abilities.

**`attack_kind`.** The words of condition effects (`melee_weapon`, `weapon`, `bound_weapon`: the one weapon the
feature is tied to, such as the pact weapon) and `ranged_weapon`, `unarmed_strike`, `monk_weapon` (an unarmed strike, a
shortsword, or a simple melee weapon without the two-handed or heavy property), `finesse_or_ranged_weapon`, `off_hand`
(the second attack of two-weapon fighting) and `spell`.

**`while`.** The words of condition effects (`source_in_line_of_sight`, `can_see_attacker`, `not_incapacitated`,
`speed_above_0`, `not_wearing_armor`, `not_wearing_heavy_armor`) and `wearing_armor`, `not_wielding_shield`,
`wielding_shield`, `not_blinded`, `not_deafened`, `can_see_effect` (the effect is one the owner can see),
`one_handed_melee_weapon_only` (a melee weapon in one hand and no other weapon), `wielding_with_two_hands` (a melee
weapon with the two-handed or versatile property, held in two hands), `not_proficient` (the roll does not already
include the proficiency bonus), `target_below_hit_point_maximum`, `target_undead_or_fiend`, `sneak_attack_eligible`
(advantage on the attack roll, or another enemy of the target within 5 feet of it that is not incapacitated and no
disadvantage), `in_dim_light_or_darkness`, `proficient` (the roll includes the proficiency bonus, the opposite of
`not_proficient`), `moving_at_most_half_speed` (the creature moves no more than half its speed on the same turn) and `conscious` (the
creature is conscious), `attacker_hit_owner_this_turn` (the creature making the attack has already hit the owner on the
same turn), `target_favored_enemy` (the target is one of the owner's favored enemies), `target_not_fiend` and `target_grappled_by_owner` (the target is a creature the owner is grappling; feats only).

The words shared with conditions are defined once, in `src/schemas/effects.ts`, which both schemas import; the
condition schema is unchanged.

## Counts

A feature whose count or size grows with the level, and that has a single record, names its `class_specific` key and
also lists the steps of that number, each with an id: `by_level`. It sits in the same object as the `class_specific` key
(in `uses`, or in the `bonus`, `speed`, `damage_die` or `recover_spell_slots` modifier that reads it):

```json
{ "class_specific": "rage_count", "by_level": [{ "id": "rage-2-uses", "level": 1, "count": 2 }, ...] }
```

A step is `{ id, level, count }` for a number or `{ id, level, dice }` for dice (`"3d6"`). `level` is the class level
at which the step starts and it holds until the next step; a step is listed only where the value changes, and not
where it is 0 (the feature has no count yet). `by_level` is derived from the Levels rows by `derive-feature-counts.ts`
(`pnpm run derive:features`), and `src/2014/tests/feature-counts.test.ts` checks it against them at every level.

The id is `<stem>-<count>-<unit>` (`<stem>-<dice>` for dice). Rage's 9999 rages at 20th level (no limit) is `count:
9999` with the id `rage-unlimited-uses`.

| Feature                               | `class_specific` key     | Ids                                                       | Steps |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| Rage `uses`                           | `rage_count`             | `rage-2-uses`, `rage-3-uses` ... `rage-unlimited-uses`    | 6     |
| Rage `bonus`                          | `rage_damage_bonus`      | `rage-2-damage`, `rage-3-damage`, `rage-4-damage`         | 3     |
| Ki `uses`                             | `ki_points`              | `ki-2-points` ... `ki-20-points`                          | 19    |
| Font of Magic `uses`                  | `sorcery_points`         | `sorcery-2-points` ... `sorcery-20-points`                | 19    |
| Sneak Attack `bonus`                  | `sneak_attack`           | `sneak-attack-1d6` ... `sneak-attack-10d6`                | 10    |
| Martial Arts `damage_die`             | `martial_arts`           | `martial-arts-1d4`, `-1d6`, `-1d8`, `-1d10`               | 4     |
| Unarmored Movement `speed`            | `unarmored_movement`     | `unarmored-movement-10-ft` ... `unarmored-movement-30-ft` | 5     |
| Arcane Recovery `recover_spell_slots` | `arcane_recovery_levels` | `arcane-recovery-1-level` ... `arcane-recovery-10-levels` | 10    |

The other `class_specific` numbers that grow with the level are not counted here, because their counts already have a
record each, each with its own id (`COUNT_EXCLUDED` in `derive-feature-counts.ts`, checked by the test): Bardic
Inspiration's die (`bardic-inspiration-d6` to `-d12`, which Peerless Skill reads as well), cleric Channel Divinity (`channel-divinity-1-rest`,
`-2-rest`, `-3-rest`), Action Surge, Indomitable, Brutal Critical, Destroy Undead, Wild Shape (three records); the table of Flexible Casting costs is not a count,
and neither is the range of the paladin auras (`aura_range`: 10 feet, then 30 feet from 18th level), which has no resource to count.
Bardic Inspiration's uses are the Charisma modifier, not a number in the Levels rows. The paladin's Channel Divinity
has one use at every level (see Judgement calls) and so no steps. Those ids are never renamed, and a split of an id
keeps the old one.

An id of `by_level` is published in `ids/feature-counts.json` and follows the id stability contract in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## `heal`

`{ dice?, bonus?, ability_modifier?, add_class_level?, class_level_multiplier?, from_uses? }`: the hit points are
`dice` plus `bonus` plus the ability modifier plus the class level (`add_class_level`) plus the class level times
`class_level_multiplier`. `from_uses: true` means as many as the owner spends from the feature's `uses`.

| Feature                         | `heal`                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Second Wind                     | `{ dice: "1d10", add_class_level: true }`                                                                                           |
| Song of Rest (4 records)        | `{ dice: "1d6" }` to `{ dice: "1d12" }`; the die is the record's, and agrees with `song_of_rest_die` of the Levels row at its level |
| Lay on Hands                    | `{ from_uses: true }`                                                                                                               |
| Channel Divinity: Preserve Life | `{ class_level_multiplier: 5 }`                                                                                                     |
| Wholeness of Body               | `{ class_level_multiplier: 3 }`                                                                                                     |

none above half a creature's hit point maximum; Song of Rest adds its die to hit dice spent at the end of a short
rest; Survivor works only at no more than half the hit points and not at 0.

## `granted_languages`

A list of the languages a feature gives. Each item is one of:

- a reference into `5e-SRD-Languages.json` (`index`, `name`, `url`);
- `{ secret_language }` for a secret language of a class, which has no record in `5e-SRD-Languages.json`: `druidic` or
  `thieves-cant`;
- `{ choose, from: "favored_enemy_languages" }` for a number of languages of the owner's choice, taken from the
  languages the favored enemies speak.

| Feature                                    | `granted_languages`                              |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Druidic                                    | `{ secret_language: "druidic" }`                 |
| Thieves' Cant                              | `{ secret_language: "thieves-cant" }`            |
| Dragon Ancestor (each of the ten dragons)  | `draconic`                                       |
| Favored Enemy (1 type, 2 types, 3 enemies) | `{ choose: 1, from: "favored_enemy_languages" }` |

The Favored Enemy text gives one language when the feature is gained and one more with each additional favored enemy
(at 6th and 14th level), and each of the three records carries the text, so each has `choose: 1`; the language need not
exist ("if they speak one at all"). The parent record Dragon Ancestor is the choice of a dragon and has no
`granted_languages`: the chosen dragon's record does. Tongue of the Sun and Moon (understanding all spoken languages)
is not a language granted and is left as text.

Parts of structured features that stay in the text:

- How long a use lasts and how it ends, where no condition carries it: Rage's end when the turn passes without an
  attack or damage, Wild Shape's hours and the rules of the beast form, the turned creature's behaviour.
- Detection and senses: Divine Sense, Primeval Awareness, Gaze of Two Minds (these have an `engine` with their cost
  and uses only).
- Movement other than speed: Step of the Wind's doubled jump, Unarmored Movement's walking on walls and water at 9th
  level.
- Lay on Hands curing disease and poison, and its 5 hit points per cure.
- Action Surge's additional action, Pact of the Blade's weapon, Deflect Missiles' catch and throw, that a Bardic
  Inspiration die goes to a creature other than the owner, and when a reaction is triggered (which creature, which
  attack).
- Mindless Rage's suspension of a charm or fear that is already on the owner, Feral Instinct's turn when surprised,
  Intimidating Presence's range, its repeated use and its 24-hour limit, Retaliation's trigger (damage from a creature
  within 5 feet), Remarkable Athlete's running long jump, Empty Body's astral projection (8 ki points; the structured
  use is the 4-ki-point invisibility), Quivering Palm's lasting effect, Stroke of Luck's turn of a miss into a hit or of
  an ability check into a 20 (it has only its cost and uses), and Tranquility's lasting until the next long rest.
- The extra 1d8 of Divine Smite against an undead or a fiend is a second `bonus` with `while:
["target_undead_or_fiend"]`; the text's limit of 5d8 applies to the slot dice only.
- Divine Intervention's percentile roll against the cleric level, the choice of the god's intervention and the 7 days
  before it can be used again after it succeeds (the structured use is the action and one use between long rests);
  Cleansing Touch's range (a touch); Holy Nimbus's bright and dim light; Signature Spell's choice of two 3rd-level
  spells, each cast once (the record has two uses, as the text allows one cast of each).
- Countercharm's performance ending when the owner is silenced or ends it, and that only the creatures that hear the
  owner benefit beyond the `must_hear_owner` of the aura (the aura has the owner's circumstance `not_incapacitated`
  only); Vanish's untraceable trail; Archdruid's use of the components while in a beast shape; Dragon Wings' armor made
  to accommodate the wings (`not_wearing_armor` is stricter: the owner wears no armor) and the damage to clothing;
  Elemental Affinity's limit of one damage roll of the spell; Draconic Presence's 24-hour immunity of a creature that
  succeeds on its saving throw; Multiattack Defense's limit to the attacks of the creature that hit the owner; Hurl
  Through Hell's removal of the target until the end of the owner's next turn, and Dark One's Own Luck's choice to use
  it after seeing the roll; the spell level of a Mystic Arcanum, which is the record's own (6th to 9th level) and is
  not a `spell` modifier, because the owner chooses the spell.

## Judgement calls

- Rage applies the `enraged` condition, which already holds its advantage, resistance and spell restrictions; the
  feature adds only the damage bonus, which the condition leaves out because it grows with the level.
- Reckless Attack, Frenzy and Sacred Weapon apply the conditions of the same name, for the same reason.
- Sneak Attack is `free` (the text says the owner can deal it) and Colossus Slayer `passive` (the text says the
  creature takes it); both are limited to once a turn with `recharge: "turn"` and one use.
- Patient Defense and Step of the Wind take actions (`dodge`, `disengage`, `dash`) rather than conditions, as the SRD
  names the actions; the `dodging` and `disengaged` conditions are what those actions leave.
- The paladin's Channel Divinity has one use (`count: 1`): its class table has no column for it.
- Natural Recovery's combined level is half the druid level rounded up, which the Levels rows do not list, so it is
  `class_level_multiplier: 0.5` with `round: "up"`; Arcane Recovery reads `arcane_recovery_levels`.
- The eldritch invocations that cast a spell at will take the spell's casting time, an action for all of them.
- Mindless Rage and Purity of Body state their immunities with `immune_to`, which the conditions `charmed`,
  `frightened` and `poisoned` already name in `immune_if_feature` (Mindless Rage only `while_condition: "enraged"`);
  the test checks both sides. Purity of Body also has immunity to poison damage.
- Empty Body's resistance to all damage but force is `types: ["all"]` with `except: ["force"]`; the text says "all
  damage but force damage".
- Relentless Rage has no `uses`: it can be used any number of times, and what changes is the save DC, so it is in the
  `dc` of the save.
- Tranquility casts sanctuary on the owner at the end of a long rest, so its `action_cost` is `rest` rather than the
  spell's casting time, and the spell's save DC is `{ ability_modifier: "wis" }` as the text says (8 + the Wisdom
  modifier + the proficiency bonus), not the owner's spell save DC.
- Retaliation is an `extra_attack` with `action_cost: "reaction"`, like Giant Killer and Horde Breaker; the creature
  that damaged the owner stays in the text.
- Wholeness of Body has an `engine` (an action, once between long rests) as well as its `heal`; Survivor has only its
  `heal`, because the healing is the whole mechanic and it takes no action.
- Slippery Mind and Diamond Soul keep `granted_proficiencies`; Diamond Soul also has an `engine` for the ki reroll.
- The paladin auras read the range from the Levels `aura_range` key, which is 10 feet from 6th level and 30 feet from
  18th level, so Aura Improvements, whose only effect is the larger range, is excluded. Aura of Devotion and Aura of
  Courage state their immunity with `immune_to` and an `aura`; the conditions `charmed` and `frightened` do not name
  them in `immune_if_feature` because they protect creatures within a range of the owner, and the test leaves
  immunities in an aura out of that check. The range of an aura is not a count with ids (`COUNT_EXCLUDED`).
- Purity of Spirit is `protection-from-evil-and-good` with `always_active: true`, as the text says the paladin is always
  under the spell's effects, and states the disadvantage of the attacks of the spell's creature types with
  `rolls_against_owner`, which the test checks against the spell's text. It is `passive`, not the spell's `action`
  casting time, because nothing is cast.
- Divine Strike has one record whose dice change at 14th level, and its Levels rows have no key for them, so the dice
  are `dice_at_class_level` (`"8": "1d8"`, `"14": "2d8"`) and carry no count ids. Improved Divine Smite, unlike
  Divine Smite, takes no spell slot and is `passive` (the text says the creature takes the damage, as for Colossus Slayer).
- Holy Nimbus has no limit on its uses in its text, so it has no `uses` or `recharge`. Its damage is `deal_damage` in an
  `aura` of 30 feet that affects `enemies`, and the advantage on saving throws against spells cast by fiends and
  undead is a `roll` with `against_spells_cast_by`; both last 1 minute (`duration`).
- The four Destroy Undead records and the Channel Divinity records at 6th and 18th level repeat the text of the first
  record of the feature, and so have the same `engine`; the `class_specific` values are the owner's totals.
- Divine Domain Improvement, Sacred Oath Improvement and Arcane Tradition Improvement repeat the text of the choice of a
  subclass and are excluded for the same reason; the Domain Spells at 7th and 9th level are excluded as the first three.
  So are the Bard College, Druid Circle, Sorcerous Origin, Ranger Archetype and Otherworldly Patron features of levels
  6 to 18 and the Circle Spells at 7th and 9th level.
- A feature whose cost or drawback the vocabulary cannot state is left out of `engine` with its reason in
  `ENGINE_EXCLUDED` instead of being recorded as if it were free: Hide in Plain Sight (a minute of preparation and a
  bonus that lasts only while the owner stays still), Eldritch Master (a minute of entreaty, which no `action_cost`
  states), Chains of Carceri (once for each creature between long rests, which a count of uses does not state),
  Fiendish Resilience (a resistance that magical and silver weapons ignore), Nature's Sanctuary (a saving throw of the
  attacker). Feral Senses, Witch Sight, Land's Stride and the Multiattack options of the Hunter have no structured
  counterpart for what they do.
- Bardic Inspiration's 10th- and 15th-level records (`bardic-inspiration-d10`, `-d12`) have the `engine` of the first two.
  Peerless Skill spends a use from the pool of Bardic Inspiration and adds the die of the owner's level (`bardic_inspiration_die`)
  to the owner's own ability check; Superior Inspiration regains one use when the owner rolls initiative with none left,
  the trigger that Perfect Self has for ki points. Song of Rest keeps its `heal` records.
- Countercharm is an `action` that gives advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed, with the
  `duration` `end_of_next_turn`, to the owner and the friendly creatures within 30 feet that can hear the owner.
- Wild Shape (CR 1 or below) has the `engine` of the two earlier Wild Shape records. Archdruid names the three records
  in `unlimited_uses`, and states that the owner ignores verbal, somatic and uncosted material components with two
  `spell_change` modifiers.
- Nature's Ward states the immunity to poison as Purity of Body does (`poisoned`, `disease` and poison damage), and its
  charm and fear immunity with `creature_types`; `immune_if_feature` of `poisoned` names it (the condition rows have no
  other change).
- Elemental Affinity is `free`: the damage bonus costs nothing, and the 1 sorcery point is the cost of the resistance
  alone, so it is on the `damage` modifier, which lasts 1 hour. Both read the damage type of the dragon ancestor the
  owner chose, from `damage_type` of the ten Dragon Ancestor records (`dragon_ancestor`). Dragon Wings is a
  `bonus_action` with no `uses`, as its text gives no limit. Draconic Presence costs 5 sorcery points from Font of
  Magic and puts `concentrating` on the owner, as the text says the aura lasts until the owner loses concentration; it
  gives `charmed` (awe) or `frightened` (fear) to the hostile creatures within 60 feet, as the two `option`s, with a
  Wisdom saving throw. The text gives no save DC, and the record uses the owner's spell save DC. Sorcerous Restoration
  regains 4 sorcery points from Font of Magic when the owner finishes a short rest, and is `passive`: nothing is used.
- The Hunter's Defensive Tactics, Multiattack and Superior Hunter's Defense are choices of one of three or two
  features and are excluded as the choice of an option; Escape the Horde is `rolls_against_owner` limited to
  opportunity attacks, Multiattack Defense a +4 `armor_class` bonus while the attacker has already hit the owner on the
  turn, Steel Will advantage on saving throws against being frightened, Evasion and Uncanny Dodge the words that the
  monk and the rogue have, and Stand Against the Tide only its `reaction` (what it forces another creature's attack
  to do stays in the text). Foe Slayer adds the Wisdom modifier to an attack roll or a damage roll against a favored
  enemy, once on each of the owner's turns: two `bonus` modifiers with the options `attack_roll` and `damage_roll`.
- The warlock invocations of 7th to 15th level cast their spell as the invocations of 1st to 5th level do: once between
  long rests with a spell slot (Bewitching Whispers, Dreadful Word, Sculptor of Flesh, Minions of Chaos) and at will
  without one (Ascendant Step and Otherworldly Leap, on the owner, Whispers of the Grave, Master of Myriad Forms,
  Visions of Distant Realms). Minions of Chaos is `passive` because conjure elemental takes a minute to cast. Lifedrinker
  is extra necrotic damage equal to the Charisma modifier (at least 1) with the pact weapon. Dark One's Own Luck adds
  1d10 to an ability check or a saving throw, once between short rests (the two options `ability_check` and
  `saving_throw`). Hurl Through Hell deals 10d10 psychic damage when the target comes back, if it is not a fiend
  (`target_returns`), once between long rests. The four Mystic Arcanum records have `uses` and `recharge` only: the
  spell is the owner's choice and no `spell` modifier can name it.
